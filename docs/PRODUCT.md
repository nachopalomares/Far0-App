# Using Far0

[Back to the overview](../README.md)

## First launch and access

Five onboarding screens introduce the library before the subscription screen. Users can continue, go back or skip the introduction, and revisit it from Settings. The onboarding does not read the clipboard or use AI.

The intended product offers one experience with monthly and annual subscriptions. A seven-day trial applies only when the store offers it and confirms eligibility. Without active access, the app presents the subscription screen while keeping settings, restoration and privacy available. Expiration does not delete the library, personal notes, offline copies or pending captures. Production purchasing is currently blocked.

## Save a link

Paste a URL into the add-link form or choose Far0 in another app's share sheet. A title and personal note are optional. The destination can be automatic, the inbox or a chosen section. A title entered by the user is preserved when web metadata is refreshed.

The share extension hands the capture to the main app for import. Metadata enrichment happens afterward, so retrieving a preview is separate from keeping the submitted link. Captures remain queued if access expires or an import cannot be saved.

## Organize and retrieve

The library includes an inbox, wishlist, books, films and series, music, custom sections and an archive. Users can move links into sections or archive them. Recently deleted items can be recovered through the app's reversible deletion flow.

Local search looks through titles, URLs, descriptions, summaries and section names, with a limited vocabulary of related terms. It is not full semantic search.

## Open a bookmark

The detail view brings together the source, title, available description, optional summary, personal note and destination. From there, users can open the original link, share it, change its organization, refresh web data, archive it or delete it.

Preview images and descriptive information depend on what the source website provides. Source-specific handling supports services including Spotify, YouTube and Google Maps, but does not guarantee extraction from every URL.

## Read offline

For compatible articles, Far0 extracts readable text and keeps a local copy. The article can then be read without a connection. Refreshing a copy preserves the previous version if the new save fails.

This stores article text. It does not download books, videos, songs or every page image. Login walls, paywalls and pages rendered entirely with JavaScript can prevent extraction.

## Optional article summaries

Summaries require separate permission before page content leaves the device. The app sends the URL, title and downloaded page HTML to its backend. The backend extracts article text and requests a summary from the AI provider. The result is saved in the local library and reused.

Personal notes are excluded. Video and music links are not summarized as media. If an article cannot be processed, the bookmark remains available with the metadata that could be retrieved.

The gallery documents one live summary of a public technology article. That demonstration does not certify the production service or the quality of summaries for other sources.

## Rediscover

Open Rediscover from the inbox header to revisit links saved more than seven days ago without a recent consultation. Archived and reversibly deleted items are excluded. “Another link” cycles through available candidates. Opening one records a consultation without automatically marking it as read or organizing it.

Suggestions use local metadata, without AI. Optional reminders run locally twice a week, between 10:00 and 21:00. Notifications are generic and do not contain bookmark titles or URLs. Rediscover can be hidden in Settings.

## Settings and privacy

Choose system, light or dark appearance; review subscription access and restoration; revisit onboarding; manage AI consent and optional usage statistics; and review local data information.

The library lives on the device, while metadata retrieval contacts source websites. AI summaries involve external processing only after permission. Optional usage statistics start disabled and exclude URLs, titles, search queries, images, notes and summaries. Withdrawing a permission stops new sends; it does not erase information already received by a provider.

## Availability

The app is in development, with a Spanish interface. It is not presented here as an App Store release or an invitation to buy a subscription. Cross-device sync and English app localization are not active.
